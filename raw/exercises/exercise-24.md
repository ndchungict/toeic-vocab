## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **B (contractor)** — "hired a local contractor to carry out the building work" = nhà thầu. *architect/tenant/janitor* sai nghĩa.
2. **D (blueprints)** — "spread the blueprints… to show the new layout" = bản vẽ thiết kế. *permit/meter/outage* sai nghĩa.
3. **A (permit)** — "obtain a building permit before work began" = giấy phép. *leak/facility/blueprint* sai nghĩa.
4. **C (zoning)** — "checked the zoning rules… a retail showroom was allowed" = quy hoạch phân khu. *structural/utilities/maintenance* sai.
5. **B (construction)** — "the space was under construction" = thi công. ⚠️ *under construction*. *inspection/outage/meter* sai.
6. **D (architect)** — "the architect redesigned the entrance" = kiến trúc sư. *contractor/janitor/tenant* sai nghĩa.
7. **A (structural)** — "not allowed to make any structural changes" = thuộc kết cấu. *portable/vacant/furnished* sai nghĩa.
8. **C (renovate)** — "worked around the clock to renovate the ground floor" = cải tạo. *relocate/adhere/inspect* sai nghĩa.
9. **B (utilities)** — "connect the utilities — power, water, and gas" = tiện ích. *blueprints/permits/meters* sai nghĩa.
10. **D (wiring)** — "all-new wiring to handle the electrical load" = hệ thống dây điện. *plumbing/insulation/scaffolding* sai nghĩa.
11. **A (outage)** — "a mistake… caused a brief power outage" = mất điện. *permit/leak/meter* sai nghĩa.
12. **C (leak)** — "fix a small leak in the ceiling" = chỗ rò rỉ. *meter/outage/permit* sai nghĩa.

### Part 6
