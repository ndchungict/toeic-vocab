## Bảng tổng hợp từ vựng

| Từ | Phiên âm (IPA) | Loại | Nghĩa | Collocation thường gặp | Ví dụ câu tiếng Anh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| release | /rɪˈliːs/ | v/n | phát hành, tung ra | release a product / an album | Aster released Aria that night. |
| review | /rɪˈvjuː/ | n | bài đánh giá | receive rave reviews | The first reviews were glowing. |

| renowned | /rɪˈnaʊnd/ | adj | nổi tiếng, danh tiếng | a renowned expert | a renowned technology critic |
| masterpiece | /ˈmɑːstəpiːs/ | n | kiệt tác | a design masterpiece | Critics called Aria a masterpiece. |
| broadcast | /ˈbrɔːdkɑːst/ | v/n | phát sóng | broadcast live ⚠️ quá khứ = **broadcast** | The demo was broadcast live. |
| journalist | /ˈdʒɜːnəlɪst/ | n | nhà báo | a freelance journalist | Journalists rushed to write about it. |

> ⚠️ **Bẫy hay gặp:** **release** (v/n) = phát hành/tung ra (sản phẩm, album, phim, thông cáo). **review** (bài đánh giá) ≠ *preview*; **critic** (người phê bình) viết **review**; **acclaim** = sự tán dương (**không đếm được**, *critical acclaim*); **renowned** (adj) ≈ famous, nổi tiếng. ⚠️ **broadcast** — quá khứ giữ nguyên *broadcast* (không *-ed*). **journalist** (nhà báo) · **editorial** (bài xã luận, n) · **publication** (ấn phẩm) · **subscription** (đăng ký). **fundraiser** = sự kiện gây quỹ; **anniversary** = lễ kỷ niệm. **sculpture** (tác phẩm) → **sculptor** (người). ⚠️ **admission fee** (phí vào cửa) khác **admission** (Ch 27 = sự cho vào/vé). **matinee** = suất ban ngày.

## Bài tập TOEIC (tự làm trước, chưa xem đáp án)

### Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences
Chọn phương án đúng (A/B/C/D) điền vào chỗ trống.

**1.** The moment the demo ended, Aster officially ______ the Aria line in Halston.
(A) reviewed (B) broadcast (C) released (D) sculpted

**2.** The live demo was ______ to viewers across the region.
(A) broadcast (B) released (C) reviewed (D) renowned

**3.** By the next morning, every ______ in the city wanted the story.
(A) critic (B) sculpture (C) editorial (D) journalist

**4.** The first ______ of Aria were overwhelmingly positive.
(A) sculptures (B) reviews (C) fundraisers (D) matinees

**5.** Even a famously harsh technology ______ had nothing but praise.
(A) journalist (B) editorial (C) critic (D) sculpture

**6.** Aria won rare critical ______ for its design and reliability.
(A) acclaim (B) subscription (C) fundraiser (D) matinee

**7.** One reviewer called the device a ______ of smart-home design.
(A) journalist (B) critic (C) subscription (D) masterpiece

**8.** The praise from such a ______ expert stunned the whole team.
(A) sold-out (B) renowned (C) contagious (D) vacant

**9.** Sign-ups poured in, and Aria ______ numbers surged overnight.
(A) admission (B) sculpture (C) subscription (D) matinee

**10.** A month later, Aster hosted a ______ to celebrate and give to charity.
(A) fundraiser (B) critic (C) journalist (D) review

**11.** The event also marked the company's twelfth ______.
(A) subscription (B) sculpture (C) review (D) anniversary

**12.** In the lobby, Rosie placed a small ______ shaped like the first Aria.

## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **C (released)** — "Aster officially released the Aria line" = phát hành, tung ra. *reviewed/broadcast/sculpted* sai nghĩa.
2. **A (broadcast)** — "the demo was broadcast to viewers across the region" = phát sóng. ⚠️ quá khứ giữ nguyên. *released/reviewed/renowned* sai.
3. **D (journalist)** — "every journalist in the city wanted the story" = nhà báo. *critic/sculpture/editorial* sai nghĩa.
4. **B (reviews)** — "the first reviews were overwhelmingly positive" = bài đánh giá. *sculptures/fundraisers/matinees* sai nghĩa.
5. **C (critic)** — "a famously harsh technology critic" = nhà phê bình. *journalist/editorial/sculpture* sai nghĩa.
6. **A (acclaim)** — "won rare critical acclaim" = sự tán dương. *subscription/fundraiser/matinee* sai nghĩa.
7. **D (masterpiece)** — "called the device a masterpiece of design" = kiệt tác. *journalist/critic/subscription* sai nghĩa.
8. **B (renowned)** — "praise from such a renowned expert" = nổi tiếng. *sold-out/contagious/vacant* sai nghĩa.
9. **C (subscription)** — "Aria subscription numbers surged overnight" = đăng ký. *admission/sculpture/matinee* sai nghĩa.
10. **A (fundraiser)** — "hosted a fundraiser to celebrate and give to charity" = sự kiện gây quỹ. *critic/journalist/review* sai nghĩa.
11. **D (anniversary)** — "marked the company's twelfth anniversary" = lễ kỷ niệm. *subscription/sculpture/review* sai nghĩa.
