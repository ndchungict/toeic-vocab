> ⚠️ **Bẫy hay gặp:** **expedite** = đẩy nhanh (đi với *shipment / delivery / order / process*) — **rất hay ra Part 5/6**. Phân biệt **carrier** (hãng vận chuyển lớn, air/sea) ≠ **courier** (chuyển phát nhanh kiện nhỏ). ⚠️ **damaged** = hỏng *do vận chuyển* (bên ngoài) ≠ **defective** = lỗi *từ sản xuất* (bản thân sản phẩm) — TOEIC rất hay bắt phân biệt. **due** = đến hạn (*payment is due*), khác **valid** = còn hiệu lực; **outstanding** ở đây = *chưa thanh toán* (khác nghĩa "xuất sắc" ở Lesson 13). **free of charge** = **at no additional cost** = miễn phí (cặp đồng nghĩa hay hoán đổi ở Part 6). **refund** (hoàn tiền) ↔ **exchange** (đổi hàng) — hai cách xử lý hàng lỗi. **surcharge** = phụ phí (*sur-* = thêm); **shipping and handling** luôn đi thành cụm.

## Bài tập TOEIC (tự làm trước, chưa xem đáp án)

### Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences
Chọn phương án đúng (A/B/C/D) điền vào chỗ trống.

**1.** Sea freight was too slow, so Ray booked an air ______ to fly the chips in.
(A) invoice (B) carrier (C) surcharge (D) refund

**2.** To meet the fixed launch date, Ray was willing to pay more to ______ the delivery.
(A) itemize (B) expedite (C) exchange (D) enclose

**3.** As soon as the order was confirmed, Halston Components agreed to ______ the goods from its warehouse.
(A) dispatch (B) refund (C) accrue (D) replenish

**4.** The delicate chips were packed in boxes clearly marked "______."
(A) overdue (B) fragile (C) outstanding (D) vacant

**5.** When the crate was opened, the team found that the ______ had been crushed in transit.
(A) packaging (B) carrier (C) surcharge (D) invoice

**6.** Nearly half of the units arrived ______ because the crate had been dropped.
(A) fragile (B) damaged (C) due (D) exempt

**7.** A few trays were ______ from the factory, with bent pins that shipping could not explain.
(A) damaged (B) frugal (C) defective (D) durable

**8.** For the express service, the courier added a ______ on top of the normal rate.
(A) refund (B) surcharge (C) discount (D) dividend

**9.** Ray asked Finance to ______ the bill so he could see every charge separately.
(A) expedite (B) dispatch (C) itemize (D) exchange

**10.** Because the goods were faulty, Ray demanded a full ______ for the damaged units.
(A) refund (B) invoice (C) carrier (D) surcharge

**11.** Payment for the shipment was ______ within ten days of delivery.
(A) fragile (B) due (C) defective (D) valid

**12.** The seller agreed to replace the faulty chips ______, so Aster paid nothing extra.
(A) in bulk (B) on backorder (C) free of charge (D) at retail

## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **B (carrier)** — "an air carrier to fly the chips in" = hãng vận chuyển. *invoice/surcharge/refund* sai nghĩa.
2. **B (expedite)** — "pay more to expedite the delivery" = đẩy nhanh. *itemize/exchange/enclose* sai nghĩa. ⚠️ collocation hay ra: *expedite + shipment/delivery/order*.
3. **A (dispatch)** — "dispatch the goods from its warehouse" = gửi hàng đi. *refund/accrue/replenish* sai nghĩa.
4. **B (fragile)** — "delicate chips… marked fragile" = dễ vỡ. *overdue/outstanding/vacant* sai nghĩa.
5. **A (packaging)** — "the packaging had been crushed in transit" = bao bì. *carrier/surcharge/invoice* sai nghĩa.
6. **B (damaged)** — "arrived damaged… the crate had been dropped" = hư hại *do vận chuyển*. *fragile/due/exempt* sai nghĩa.
7. **C (defective)** — "defective from the factory… bent pins" = lỗi *từ sản xuất*. ⚠️ khác *damaged* (lỗi do vận chuyển). *frugal/durable* sai nghĩa.
8. **B (surcharge)** — "added a surcharge on top of the normal rate" = phụ phí. *refund/discount/dividend* sai nghĩa.
9. **C (itemize)** — "itemize the bill… every charge separately" = liệt kê chi tiết. *expedite/dispatch/exchange* sai nghĩa.
10. **A (refund)** — "demanded a full refund for the damaged units" = tiền hoàn lại. *invoice/carrier/surcharge* sai nghĩa.
11. **B (due)** — "payment was due within ten days" = đến hạn. ⚠️ *valid* = còn hiệu lực (khác). *fragile/defective* sai nghĩa.
12. **C (free of charge)** — "replace the faulty chips… paid nothing extra" = miễn phí. *in bulk/on backorder/at retail* sai nghĩa.

### Part 6
13. **B (expedite)** — "asked us to expedite the delivery… by air… within 48 hours" = đẩy nhanh. *itemize/exchange/reimburse* sai.
14. **C (surcharge)** — "a rush surcharge added to the shipping and handling fee" = phụ phí. *refund/discount/dividend* sai.
15. **B (fragile)** — "labelled fragile, as the contents are easily broken" = dễ vỡ. *vacant/overdue/defective* sai.
16. **A (track)** — "a tracking number… track the shipment at any time" = theo dõi lô hàng. *dispatch/replenish/audit* sai.
17. **B (shipment)** — "part of your recent shipment reached you in poor condition" = lô hàng. *surcharge/refund/carrier* sai.
18. **C (defective)** — "several units were defective when they left our plant" = lỗi *từ nhà máy*. *fragile/frugal/due* sai.
19. **C (free of charge)** — "send replacements free of charge" = miễn phí. *on backorder/in bulk/at retail* sai.
20. **A (at no additional cost)** — "cover the return shipping at no additional cost" = không mất thêm phí. *out of stock/in advance/on schedule* sai.

### Part 7
21. **B** — chi phí cao vì "flown in and **expedited** rather than sea freight… added a significant **surcharge**". (A)/(C)/(D) sai.
22. **A** — hai loại lỗi: "**damaged in transit**" vs "**defective from the factory**". ⚠️ phân biệt *damaged* (vận chuyển) ≠ *defective* (sản xuất). (B)/(C)/(D) sai.
23. **C** — Aster thương lượng được "replacements **at no additional cost**". (A)/(B)/(D) sai.
24. **B** — giữ dây chuyền chạy bằng cách "rushed a **replacement shipment**… paid a premium to **expedite** delivery". (A)/(C)/(D) sai.
25. **A** — đòi được "a **refund** on parts that arrived damaged". (B)/(C)/(D) sai.
26. **C** — vẫn lo vì "replacement chips came from **older clearance stock that was never fully tested**" → ngòi nổ khủng hoảng chất lượng Ch 17–18. (A)/(B)/(D) sai.
