## Bảng tổng hợp từ vựng

| Từ | Phiên âm (IPA) | Loại | Nghĩa | Collocation thường gặp | Ví dụ câu tiếng Anh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plant | /plɑːnt/ | n | nhà máy | a manufacturing plant ⚠️ **không** phải "cái cây" | The Eastport plant ran day and night. |
| assembly line | /əˈsembli laɪn/ | n | dây chuyền lắp ráp | work **on** the assembly line | Aria units moved down the assembly line. |

| assembly | /əˈsembli/ | n | việc lắp ráp | final assembly | Final assembly took dozens of steps. |
| component | /kəmˈpəʊnənt/ | n | linh kiện | replace a component | The new chip was a key component. |
| raw materials | /rɔː məˈtɪəriəlz/ | n | nguyên liệu thô | source raw materials | Raw materials were fed in at the start. |
| machinery | /məˈʃiːnəri/ | n | máy móc (nói chung) | heavy machinery ⚠️ danh từ **không đếm được** | Heavy machinery stamped the casings. |
| equipment | /ɪˈkwɪpmənt/ | n | thiết bị | ⚠️ **không đếm được** — không có *equipments* | The welding equipment was precise. |

| productivity | /ˌprɒdʌkˈtɪvəti/ | n | năng suất | increase productivity | They pushed productivity to the limit. |
| efficiency | /ɪˈfɪʃnsi/ | n | hiệu suất | improve efficiency → **efficient** | Small changes improved efficiency. |
| capacity | /kəˈpæsəti/ | n | công suất, sức chứa | operate **at** full capacity | The plant ran at full capacity. |
| streamline | /ˈstriːmlaɪn/ | v | tinh gọn quy trình | streamline the process | They streamlined a few wasteful steps. |
| shift | /ʃɪft/ | n | ca làm việc | work the night shift | The factory added a night shift. |
| foreman | /ˈfɔːmən/ | n | quản đốc, đốc công | the shift foreman | Joe, the foreman, ran the floor. |
| supervise | /ˈsuːpəvaɪz/ | v | giám sát | → **supervisor** | Joe supervised every shift himself. |

> ⚠️ **Bẫy hay gặp:** **machinery** & **equipment** là danh từ **KHÔNG đếm được** — không bao giờ thêm *-s*, chia động từ số ít, dùng *a piece of / much equipment* (❌ *equipments*). Nhóm chỉ số sản xuất **output / productivity / efficiency / capacity** rất hay ra Part 5 (chọn đúng danh từ theo nghĩa). Họ từ: **assemble** (v) → **assembly** (n) → **assembly line** (n); **supervise** (v) → **supervisor** (n, Phụ lục A). **capacity** đi với cụm *at full capacity*. **plant** = *nhà máy* (không phải "cây"); **shift** = *ca làm việc* (không phải "dịch chuyển"); **streamline** = *tinh gọn quy trình* (không phải "dòng suối").

## Bài tập TOEIC (tự làm trước, chưa xem đáp án)

### Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences
Chọn phương án đúng (A/B/C/D) điền vào chỗ trống.

**1.** The Eastport ______ ran day and night to meet the Halston deadline.
(A) plant (B) shift (C) output (D) foreman

**2.** Aria units moved slowly down the ______ as workers added parts at each station.
(A) capacity (B) assembly line (C) machinery (D) efficiency

**3.** So that machines could handle the dull work, several repetitive steps had been ______.
(A) supervised (B) assembled (C) automated (D) inspected

**4.** Joe, the ______, walked the floor and checked on every station personally.
(A) component (B) foreman (C) output (D) shift

**5.** With demand so high, the factory operated at full ______.
(A) capacity (B) efficiency (C) assembly (D) warehouse

**6.** Management added a night ______ so the line could run around the clock.
(A) plant (B) shift (C) output (D) foreman

**7.** Ray monitored the hourly ______ to see how many units the line produced.
(A) capacity (B) foreman (C) output (D) shift

**8.** By removing a few wasteful movements, the team managed to ______ the process.
(A) supervise (B) assemble (C) streamline (D) automate

**9.** Workers ______ the boards by fitting each chip and connector into place.
(A) assembled (B) supervised (C) streamlined (D) dispatched

**10.** Heavy ______ at the front of the line stamped the metal casings.
(A) machinery (B) shifts (C) outputs (D) components

**11.** The new chip was the most important ______ inside each Aria unit.
(A) capacity (B) component (C) foreman (D) efficiency

**12.** Small process changes helped raise both output and ______ on the line.
(A) machinery (B) plant (C) efficiency (D) shift

## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **A (plant)** — "The Eastport plant ran day and night" = nhà máy. *shift/output/foreman* sai nghĩa. ⚠️ plant ≠ "cây".
2. **B (assembly line)** — "moved down the assembly line… at each station" = dây chuyền lắp ráp. *capacity/machinery/efficiency* sai nghĩa.
3. **C (automated)** — "machines could handle the dull work… steps had been automated" = tự động hóa. *supervised/assembled/inspected* sai nghĩa.
4. **B (foreman)** — "walked the floor and checked every station" = quản đốc. *component/output/shift* sai nghĩa.
5. **A (capacity)** — "operated at full capacity" = hết công suất. ⚠️ cụm cố định *at full capacity*. *efficiency/assembly/warehouse* sai.
6. **B (shift)** — "a night shift… run around the clock" = ca làm việc. *plant/output/foreman* sai nghĩa.
7. **C (output)** — "how many units the line produced" = sản lượng. *capacity/foreman/shift* sai nghĩa.
8. **C (streamline)** — "removing wasteful movements… streamline the process" = tinh gọn quy trình. *supervise/assemble/automate* sai nghĩa.
9. **A (assembled)** — "fitting each chip and connector into place" = lắp ráp. *supervised/streamlined/dispatched* sai nghĩa.
10. **A (machinery)** — "heavy machinery… stamped the casings" = máy móc. ⚠️ không đếm được (loại các đáp án số nhiều). *shifts/outputs/components* sai.
11. **B (component)** — "the most important component inside each unit" = linh kiện. *capacity/foreman/efficiency* sai nghĩa.
12. **C (efficiency)** — "raise both output and efficiency" = hiệu suất. *machinery/plant/shift* sai nghĩa.

### Part 6
13. **A (at full capacity)** — "run at full capacity around the clock" = hết công suất. *out of stock/free of charge/on backorder* sai ngữ cảnh.
14. **B (conveyor belt)** — "keep the conveyor belt moving… don't let units pile up between stations" = băng chuyền. *foreman/shift/output* sai.
15. **C (supervisor)** — "follow their supervisor's instructions" = quản lý trực tiếp (họ từ của *supervise*). *supplier/tenant/applicant* sai nghĩa.
16. **A (productivity)** — "raise productivity without sacrificing quality" = năng suất. *machinery/warehouse/raw materials* sai nghĩa.
17. **A (Raw materials)** — "raw materials and components arrive… so nothing sits idle" = nguyên liệu thô. *Shifts/Outputs/Foremen* sai nghĩa.
18. **B (assemble)** — "operators assemble each unit by hand where machines cannot" = lắp ráp. *supervise/streamline/dispatch* sai.
19. **C (assembly)** — "final assembly is completed at the last station" = việc lắp ráp. *capacity/efficiency/plant* sai nghĩa.
20. **A (equipment)** — "well-maintained equipment… breakdowns rare" = thiết bị. ⚠️ không đếm được. *shifts/outputs/components* sai.

### Part 7
21. **B** — nâng sản lượng bằng cách "ran **at full capacity** and added a **shift**", đẩy output kỷ lục. (A)/(C)/(D) sai/trái.
22. **C** — cải thiện hiệu suất nhờ "**streamlined** several steps and **automated** the most repetitive work… without adding staff". (A)/(B)/(D) sai.
23. **B** — Joe cảnh báo "quality can slip when people are **pushed too hard** for too long". (A)/(C)/(D) sai.
24. **B** — Aster "driven its Eastport **plant to full capacity**" cho màn ra mắt Halston. (A)/(C)/(D) sai/trái.
25. **B** — căng thẳng lao động trở lại vì "workers are logging **long overtime shifts**" (nối tuyến Ch 8). (A)/(C)/(D) sai.
26. **C** — dấu hiệu cảnh báo: "units built with **hastily sourced replacement parts** are… showing odd behavior" → ngòi nổ QC Ch 18. (A)/(B)/(D) sai.
