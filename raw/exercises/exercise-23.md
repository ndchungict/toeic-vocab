## Bảng tổng hợp từ vựng

> ⚠️ **Bẫy hay gặp:** **lease** (hợp đồng thuê dài hạn, *sign a lease*) khác **rent** (tiền thuê / hành động thuê, *pay the rent*). **landlord** (chủ nhà) ↔ **tenant** (người thuê). **vacant** (còn trống) → **vacancy** (n). ⚠️ **appraisal** ở đây = *định giá bất động sản* (a property appraisal), **KHÁC** nghĩa *đánh giá nhân viên* (performance appraisal — Lesson 3). **property** ≈ **real estate** = bất động sản (*a real estate agent*). ⚠️ **premises** luôn ở **số nhiều** (*on the premises* = trong khuôn viên). **relocate** đi với **to**. ⚠️ **security deposit** = *tiền đặt cọc thuê nhà* (khác *deposit* = gửi tiền ngân hàng — Lesson 13). **occupancy** = sức chứa / tỷ lệ lấp đầy.

## Bài tập TOEIC (tự làm trước, chưa xem đáp án)

### Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences
Chọn phương án đúng (A/B/C/D) điền vào chỗ trống.

**1.** Aster urgently needed ______ in Halston for a showroom, a warehouse, and its servers.
(A) tenants (B) rent (C) premises (D) appraisal

**2.** Karen recommended a local ______ agent who knew the downtown market well.
(A) real estate (B) tenant (C) landlord (D) occupancy

**3.** One unit was ______, with no current occupant, so they could take it immediately.
(A) furnished (B) spacious (C) rented (D) vacant

**4.** The property near Brightway was bright and ______, with plenty of room.

(A) vacant (B) spacious (C) furnished (D) portable

(A) lease (B) tenant (C) appraisal (D) rent

**7.** The ______ required a large deposit before handing over the keys.
(A) tenant (B) occupancy (C) appraisal (D) landlord

**8.** By signing the agreement, Aster officially became the building's new ______.
(A) landlord (B) tenant (C) appraisal (D) premises

**9.** In addition to the first month's rent, they paid a substantial ______.
(A) real estate (B) occupancy (C) security deposit (D) appraisal

**10.** Buying would have required a formal ______ to establish the property's value.
(A) appraisal (B) tenant (C) lease (D) landlord

**11.** Priya planned to ______ several key staff from Marlowe to Halston.
(A) furnish (B) lease (C) rent (D) relocate

**12.** Ray checked the building's maximum ______ to be sure it could fit all three functions.

## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **C (premises)** — "needed premises… for a showroom, warehouse and servers" = mặt bằng. *tenants/rent/appraisal* sai nghĩa.
2. **A (real estate)** — "a real estate agent who knew the market" = bất động sản. *tenant/landlord/occupancy* sai nghĩa.
3. **D (vacant)** — "vacant, with no current occupant… take it immediately" = còn trống. *furnished/spacious/rented* sai nghĩa.
4. **B (spacious)** — "bright and spacious, with plenty of room" = rộng rãi. *vacant/furnished/obsolete* sai nghĩa.
5. **C (furnished)** — "already furnished… no need to buy desks or chairs" = có sẵn nội thất. *vacant/spacious/portable* sai nghĩa.
6. **A (lease)** — "signed a three-year lease on the space" = hợp đồng thuê dài hạn. ⚠️ *sign a lease* (không phải *rent*). *tenant/appraisal/rent* sai.
7. **D (landlord)** — "the landlord required a deposit before the keys" = chủ nhà. *tenant/occupancy/appraisal* sai nghĩa.
8. **B (tenant)** — "became the building's new tenant" = người thuê. *landlord/appraisal/premises* sai nghĩa.
9. **C (security deposit)** — "the first month's rent plus a substantial security deposit" = tiền đặt cọc. *real estate/occupancy/appraisal* sai nghĩa.
10. **A (appraisal)** — "a formal appraisal to establish the property's value" = sự định giá. ⚠️ định giá BĐS (khác đánh giá nhân viên L3). *tenant/lease/landlord* sai.
11. **D (relocate)** — "relocate several staff from Marlowe to Halston" = điều chuyển. *furnish/lease/rent* sai nghĩa.
12. **B (occupancy)** — "checked the maximum occupancy… fit all three functions" = sức chứa. *appraisal/landlord/deposit* sai nghĩa.

### Part 6
13. **B (furnished)** — "a fully furnished office ready to use" = có sẵn nội thất. *vacant/obsolete/portable* sai nghĩa.
14. **D (rent)** — "the monthly rent is higher" = tiền thuê. *appraisal/tenant/landlord* sai nghĩa.
15. **A (spacious)** — "a larger, spacious unit near Brightway" = rộng rãi. *vacant/furnished/durable* sai nghĩa.
16. **C (vacant)** — "currently vacant and available at once" = còn trống. *furnished/spacious/rented* sai nghĩa.
17. **C (property)** — "secured a commercial property in central Halston" = bất động sản. *tenant/appraisal/rent* sai nghĩa.
18. **A (lease)** — "signed a three-year lease" = hợp đồng thuê. *landlord/occupancy/appraisal* sai nghĩa.
19. **D (tenant)** — "now the official tenant of the building" = người thuê. *landlord/appraisal/premises* sai nghĩa.
20. **B (premises)** — "no smoking anywhere on the premises" = khuôn viên. ⚠️ *on the premises*. *occupancy/rent/real estate* sai.

### Part 7
21. **C** — chọn thuê thay vì mua vì "the **launch date is fixed**" (phải nhanh). (A)/(B)/(D) sai/không nêu.
22. **A** — giữ được mặt bằng: "**signed a lease**, paid a **security deposit**, and became… **tenants**". (B)/(C)/(D) sai.
23. **D** — chỗ này còn trống trơn, "must be **fitted out** before it can open". (A)/(B)/(C) sai.
24. **B** — Aster "**leased a prime commercial site** in central Halston". (A)/(C)/(D) sai.
25. **A** — thuê thay vì mua "in order to **move quickly**". (B)/(C)/(D) sai.
26. **C** — thách thức kế tiếp: "a tight race to **renovate** it… showroom, warehouse, power and utilities" → dẫn Ch 24. (A)/(B)/(D) sai.
