## Bảng tổng hợp từ vựng

| Từ | Phiên âm (IPA) | Loại | Nghĩa | Collocation thường gặp | Ví dụ câu tiếng Anh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| warranty | /ˈwɒrənti/ | n | (chế độ) bảo hành | be under warranty; a two-year warranty | The appliance is still under warranty, so repairs are free. |
| guarantee | /ˌɡærənˈtiː/ | n/v | sự bảo đảm; bảo đảm | a money-back guarantee | The product comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee. |
| defect | /ˈdiːfekt/ | n | lỗi sản phẩm | a manufacturing defect → defective (adj) | Any unit with a defect will be replaced free of charge. |
| faulty | /ˈfɔːlti/ | adj | bị lỗi, hỏng | a faulty part / component | The technician removed the faulty component and fitted a new one. |
| repair | /rɪˈpeə(r)/ | v/n | sửa chữa | carry out / make a repair; free repairs | The warranty covers free repairs for two years. |
| replace | /rɪˈpleɪs/ | v | thay thế | replace a faulty part → replacement (n) | If it cannot be fixed, we will replace the whole unit. |
| expire | /ɪkˈspaɪə(r)/ | v | hết hạn | the warranty / contract expires | The warranty expires two years after purchase. |
| coverage | /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ/ | n | phạm vi bảo hành | full / extended coverage | The plan offers full coverage for both parts and labor. |
| strategy | /ˈstrætədʒi/ | n | chiến lược | develop / adopt a strategy → strategic (adj) | The team agreed on a clear strategy before the launch. |
| objective | /əbˈdʒektɪv/ | n | mục tiêu | meet / achieve an objective | Each department was given a measurable objective. |
| initiative | /ɪˈnɪʃətɪv/ | n | sáng kiến, kế hoạch mới | launch / lead an initiative | The company launched a new green initiative this year. |
| feasible | /ˈfiːzəbl/ | adj | khả thi | a feasible plan / solution | The engineers confirmed that the deadline was feasible. |
| deadline | /ˈdedlaɪn/ | n | hạn chót | meet / miss / extend a deadline | The team worked overtime to meet the tight deadline. |
| priority | /praɪˈɒrəti/ | n | ưu tiên | a top priority → prioritize (v) | Product safety is the company's top priority. |
| projection | /prəˈdʒekʃn/ | n | dự báo (số liệu) | sales / financial projections | The sales projections for next year look promising. |
| benchmark | /ˈbentʃmɑːk/ | n | chuẩn so sánh | set / meet a benchmark | Industry benchmarks help a company measure its performance. |
| assess | /əˈses/ | v | đánh giá | assess the risks → assessment (n) | Managers met to assess the risks of the new plan. |
| implement | /ˈɪmplɪment/ | v | triển khai | implement a plan / policy → implementation (n) | It is hard to implement a plan without enough staff. |
| contingency plan | /kənˈtɪndʒənsi plæn/ | n | kế hoạch dự phòng | have / prepare a contingency plan | They prepared a contingency plan in case parts ran short. |

> ⚠️ **Bẫy hay gặp:** **repair** (sửa) ≠ **replace** (thay mới). **faulty** (adj) / **defect** (n) / *defective* (adj) — cùng gốc "lỗi". **warranty** (bảo hành, thường là văn bản) ≈ **guarantee** (sự bảo đảm nói chung). **expire** = hết hạn (warranty/contract). **objective** (mục tiêu) ≠ *objection* (sự phản đối). **feasible** là **adj** (khả thi). **assess** → **assessment**; **implement** → **implementation**.

## Bài tập TOEIC (tự làm trước, chưa xem đáp án)

### Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences
Chọn phương án đúng (A/B/C/D) điền vào chỗ trống.

**1.** The refrigerator is still under ______, so Aster will fix it at no cost.
(A) coverage (B) warranty (C) deadline (D) benchmark

**2.** The product comes with a 30-day money-back ______ if customers are not satisfied.
(A) priority (B) projection (C) guarantee (D) strategy

**3.** The technician quickly identified the ______ component and swapped it for a new one.
(A) faulty (B) feasible (C) strategic (D) prompt

**4.** Under the two-year warranty, all ______ are carried out free of charge.
(A) objectives (B) deadlines (C) projections (D) repairs

**5.** If the device cannot be fixed, the company will ______ it with a brand-new unit.
(A) assess (B) replace (C) expire (D) implement

**6.** Customers should register their product before the warranty ______ at the end of the second year.
(A) implements (B) assesses (C) expires (D) replaces

**7.** Before spending a single dollar, the team agreed on a clear marketing ______.
(A) strategy (B) warranty (C) defect (D) coverage

**8.** Each department was given one measurable ______ to achieve by the end of the quarter.
(A) warranty (B) guarantee (C) repair (D) objective

**9.** The engineers studied the timeline and confirmed that the plan was ______.
(A) faulty (B) contingent (C) feasible (D) overdue

**10.** Because the ______ was so tight, the staff had to work late every evening.
(A) coverage (B) deadline (C) benchmark (D) warranty

**11.** Ensuring that the product is safe to use is the company's top ______.
(A) priority (B) initiative (C) projection (D) defect

**12.** A committee was formed to ______ the risks before the plan was approved.
(A) expire (B) replace (C) implement (D) assess

## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **B (warranty)** — "be under warranty" = còn trong thời hạn bảo hành; hợp với "fix it at no cost". *coverage* (phạm vi) không đi với "under" kiểu này; *deadline/benchmark* sai nghĩa.
2. **C (guarantee)** — "a money-back guarantee" = cam kết hoàn tiền. *priority/projection/strategy* sai nghĩa.
3. **A (faulty)** — "a faulty component" = linh kiện bị lỗi; hợp với "swapped it for a new one". *feasible* (khả thi), *strategic* (thuộc chiến lược), *prompt* (nhanh) sai nghĩa.
4. **D (repairs)** — "repairs are carried out free of charge" = việc sửa chữa được thực hiện miễn phí. *objectives/deadlines/projections* không "carried out free" theo nghĩa bảo hành.
5. **B (replace)** — "replace it with a brand-new unit" = thay bằng máy mới. *assess* (đánh giá), *expire* (hết hạn), *implement* (triển khai) sai nghĩa. ⚠️ replace (thay) ≠ repair (sửa).
6. **C (expires)** — "the warranty expires at the end of year two" = bảo hành hết hạn. *implements/assesses/replaces* sai nghĩa.
7. **A (strategy)** — "a clear marketing strategy" = chiến lược marketing rõ ràng. *warranty/defect/coverage* sai nghĩa.
8. **D (objective)** — "a measurable objective to achieve" = mục tiêu đo lường được để đạt. *warranty/guarantee/repair* sai nghĩa.
9. **C (feasible)** — "confirmed that the plan was feasible" = xác nhận kế hoạch khả thi. *faulty* (lỗi), *contingent* (phụ thuộc), *overdue* (quá hạn) sai nghĩa. ⚠️ feasible là tính từ.
10. **B (deadline)** — "the deadline was so tight" = hạn chót quá gấp; hợp với "work late every evening". *coverage/benchmark/warranty* sai nghĩa.
11. **A (priority)** — "the top priority" = ưu tiên hàng đầu. *initiative/projection/defect* sai nghĩa.
12. **D (assess)** — "assess the risks" = đánh giá rủi ro. *expire/replace/implement* sai nghĩa.

### Part 6
13. **A (initiative)** — "lead its own initiative" = mỗi phòng ban dẫn dắt một sáng kiến riêng. *defect/warranty/repair* sai ngữ cảnh kế hoạch.
14. **C (projections)** — "revenue projections for the next four quarters" = dự báo doanh thu. *benchmark/coverage/guarantees* sai nghĩa.
15. **B (benchmark)** — "measured against an industry benchmark" = đối chiếu với chuẩn so sánh ngành. *objective/deadline/priority* sai nghĩa.
16. **D (implement)** — "unless we can implement it on time" = trừ phi triển khai được đúng hạn. *expire/replace/assess* sai nghĩa.
17. **A (defect)** — "develops a defect caused by manufacturing" = phát sinh lỗi do sản xuất. *benchmark/priority/strategy* sai nghĩa.
18. **C (coverage)** — "full coverage includes both parts and labor" = phạm vi bảo hành gồm cả linh kiện & công. *deadline/objective/projection* sai nghĩa.
19. **B (contingency plan)** — "a contingency plan ready to help… if a large-scale problem arises" = kế hoạch dự phòng. *benchmark/guarantee/initiative* sai nghĩa.

### Part 7
20. **D** — bài nói Aster **dùng chế độ bảo hành mạnh để thu hút khách còn e ngại** ("betting that a strong warranty will win over cautious buyers"). (A)/(B)/(C) đều trái nội dung.
21. **C** — chuyên gia cảnh báo **bảo hành hào phóng sẽ rủi ro nếu sản phẩm không bền** ("only sustainable if the product is genuinely durable… if too many units fail, the cost… could be severe"). (A)/(B)/(D) không đúng.
22. **A (long-lasting)** — "durable" = bền, **lâu hỏng**. *expensive/attractive/lightweight* sai nghĩa.
23. **B** — email yêu cầu coi **an toàn & chất lượng sản phẩm** là ưu tiên số một ("treat product safety and quality as our top priority"). (A)/(C)/(D) không phải trọng tâm.
24. **D (a contingency plan)** — Priya yêu cầu mỗi lead chuẩn bị **kế hoạch dự phòng** ("prepare a short contingency plan"). (A)/(B)/(C) không được nêu.
25. **C (evaluated)** — "assessed the risks" ≈ **evaluated** (đã đánh giá). *ignored/increased/postponed* sai nghĩa.
