## Bảng tổng hợp từ vựng

| Từ | Phiên âm (IPA) | Loại | Nghĩa | Collocation thường gặp | Ví dụ câu tiếng Anh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| appointment | /əˈpɔɪntmənt/ | n | cuộc hẹn (khám) | make / cancel an appointment | Priya made an urgent appointment. |
| clinic | /ˈklɪnɪk/ | n | phòng khám | a walk-in clinic | She took him to a nearby clinic. |
| physician | /fɪˈzɪʃn/ | n | bác sĩ | consult a physician | Another physician could be consulted. |

| patient | /ˈpeɪʃnt/ | n | bệnh nhân | treat a patient ⚠️ *adj* = kiên nhẫn | Ben was now the patient. |
| symptom | /ˈsɪmptəm/ | n | triệu chứng | show symptoms **of** sth | He described his symptoms. |
| diagnose | /ˈdaɪəɡnəʊz/ | v | chẩn đoán | be diagnosed **with** → **diagnosis** | The doctor diagnosed exhaustion. |
| prescribe | /prɪˈskraɪb/ | v | kê đơn | prescribe medication → **prescription** | She prescribed rest and fluids. |
| pharmacy | /ˈfɑːməsi/ | n | hiệu thuốc | pick it up at the pharmacy → **pharmacist** | He got the pills at the pharmacy. |

| injury | /ˈɪndʒəri/ | n | chấn thương | a work-related injury | He had a minor wrist injury. |
| surgery | /ˈsɜːdʒəri/ | n | ca phẫu thuật | undergo surgery → **surgeon** | It was not serious enough for surgery. |
| ward | /wɔːd/ | n | khoa, phòng bệnh | the maternity ward | He rested a few hours in a ward. |
| referral | /rɪˈfɜːrəl/ | n | giấy giới thiệu chuyên khoa | get / write a referral | She wrote a referral to a specialist. |
| recover | /rɪˈkʌvə(r)/ | v | hồi phục | recover **from** an illness | He would recover at home. |
| relieve | /rɪˈliːv/ | v | làm giảm (đau, mệt) | relieve pain → **relief** (n) | Fluids helped relieve the fatigue. |
| side effect | /ˈsaɪd ɪfekt/ | n | tác dụng phụ | experience side effects | Drowsiness is a common side effect. |

> ⚠️ **Bẫy hay gặp:** **patient** (n = bệnh nhân) khác **patient** (adj = kiên nhẫn). **diagnose** (v) → **diagnosis** (n); *be diagnosed **with***. ⚠️ **prescribe** (kê đơn) → **prescription**; đừng lẫn với *describe* (mô tả). Nhóm "người khám": **physician** (bác sĩ) · **practitioner** (đa khoa/GP) · **specialist** (chuyên khoa) · **surgeon** (phẫu thuật). **symptom + of**; **recover + from**; **relieve** → **relief** (n). **referral** (giấy giới thiệu) — họ với *refer*. ⚠️ **ward** (khoa/phòng bệnh) khác **award** (giải thưởng — Lesson 4). **side effect** thường ở số nhiều (*side effects*).

## Bài tập TOEIC (tự làm trước, chưa xem đáp án)

### Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences
Chọn phương án đúng (A/B/C/D) điền vào chỗ trống.

**1.** Priya rushed Ben to a nearby ______ after he collapsed.
(A) ward (B) pharmacy (C) clinic (D) referral

**2.** The doctor asked Ben to describe his ______, such as dizziness and a racing heart.
(A) symptoms (B) dosages (C) referrals (D) surgeries

**3.** After some tests, the doctor ______ exhaustion and dehydration.
(A) prescribed (B) relieved (C) recovered (D) diagnosed

**4.** The sprain was minor and not serious enough to require ______.
(A) a referral (B) surgery (C) a symptom (D) a dosage

**5.** The physician ______ medication for the pain and told him to rest.
(A) diagnosed (B) recovered (C) prescribed (D) relieved

**6.** Ben took the prescription to the ______ to pick up his medicine.
(A) pharmacy (B) ward (C) clinic (D) referral

**7.** The pharmacist explained the ______: one tablet twice a day, after meals.
(A) symptom (B) injury (C) surgery (D) dosage

**8.** Drowsiness is a common ______ of the medication, so he should not drive.
(A) treatment (B) side effect (C) referral (D) dosage

**9.** To be safe, the doctor wrote a ______ so Ben could see a heart specialist.
(A) pharmacy (B) dosage (C) referral (D) ward

**10.** With a few days' rest, Ben would fully ______ at home.
(A) recover (B) prescribe (C) diagnose (D) relieve

**11.** Priya called to make an urgent ______ as soon as Ben fainted.
(A) injury (B) symptom (C) ward (D) appointment

**12.** At the clinic, the exhausted trainee was now simply another ______.
(A) physician (B) patient (C) specialist (D) practitioner

## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **C (clinic)** — "rushed Ben to a nearby clinic" = phòng khám. *ward/pharmacy/referral* sai nghĩa.
2. **A (symptoms)** — "describe his symptoms… dizziness and a racing heart" = triệu chứng. *dosages/referrals/surgeries* sai nghĩa.
3. **D (diagnosed)** — "the doctor diagnosed exhaustion and dehydration" = chẩn đoán. *prescribed/relieved/recovered* sai nghĩa.
4. **B (surgery)** — "the sprain was minor… not serious enough to require surgery" = phẫu thuật. *a referral/a symptom/a dosage* sai nghĩa.
5. **C (prescribed)** — "prescribed medication for the pain" = kê đơn. *diagnosed/recovered/relieved* sai nghĩa.
6. **A (pharmacy)** — "took the prescription to the pharmacy" = hiệu thuốc. *ward/clinic/referral* sai nghĩa.
7. **D (dosage)** — "explained the dosage: one tablet twice a day" = liều lượng. *symptom/injury/surgery* sai nghĩa.
8. **B (side effect)** — "drowsiness is a common side effect" = tác dụng phụ. *treatment/referral/dosage* sai nghĩa.
9. **C (referral)** — "wrote a referral so Ben could see a specialist" = giấy giới thiệu. *pharmacy/dosage/ward* sai nghĩa.
10. **A (recover)** — "with rest, Ben would fully recover" = hồi phục. *prescribe/diagnose/relieve* sai nghĩa.
11. **D (appointment)** — "make an urgent appointment as soon as Ben fainted" = cuộc hẹn khám. *injury/symptom/ward* sai nghĩa.
12. **B (patient)** — "the exhausted trainee was now… another patient" = bệnh nhân. *physician/specialist/practitioner* sai nghĩa.

### Part 6
