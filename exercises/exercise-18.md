## Bảng tổng hợp từ vựng

| Từ | Phiên âm (IPA) | Loại | Nghĩa | Collocation thường gặp | Ví dụ câu tiếng Anh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| quality control (QC) | /ˈkwɒləti kənˈtrəʊl/ | n | kiểm soát chất lượng | pass / fail quality control | The batch failed quality control. |
| inspect | /ɪnˈspekt/ | v | kiểm tra kỹ | → **inspector**, **inspection** | QC inspected every unit in the batch. |
| defect | /ˈdiːfekt/ | n | lỗi sản phẩm | detect a defect → **defective** | The chips had a serious defect. |
| flaw | /flɔː/ | n | khuyết điểm | a design flaw → **flawless** | It was not just a minor flaw. |
| durable | /ˈdjʊərəbl/ | adj | bền | a durable material → **durability** | The old chips were not durable enough. |
| standard | /ˈstændəd/ | n | tiêu chuẩn | meet safety / quality standards | No unit met the safety standard. |
| malfunction | /ˌmælˈfʌŋkʃn/ | n/v | trục trặc, hỏng hóc | a mechanical malfunction | Many units malfunctioned under load. |
| breakdown | /ˈbreɪkdaʊn/ | n | sự hỏng hóc | a machine breakdown | One test unit suffered a total breakdown. |
| halt | /hɔːlt/ | v/n | dừng lại | halt production | Ray halted the entire line. |
| recall | /rɪˈkɔːl/ | n/v | sự thu hồi sản phẩm | issue a recall ⚠️ *cũng = nhớ lại* | A recall would ruin the brand. |
| hazard | /ˈhæzəd/ | n | mối nguy hiểm | a safety / fire hazard → **hazardous** | Overheating chips are a fire hazard. |
| protective gear | /prəˈtektɪv ɡɪə(r)/ | n | đồ bảo hộ | wear protective gear | Workers wore protective gear. |
| warehouse | /ˈweəhaʊs/ | n | nhà kho | store goods in a warehouse | The batch was sealed in the warehouse. |
| batch | /bætʃ/ | n | lô sản xuất | a defective batch | The whole batch was defective. |
| outsource | /ˈaʊtsɔːs/ | v | thuê ngoài | outsource production | They refused to outsource the fix. |
| precision | /prɪˈsɪʒn/ | n | độ chính xác | with great precision → **precise** | Sophia examined it with precision. |

> ⚠️ **Bẫy hay gặp:** **defect** (n, lỗi) → **defective** (adj); đừng lẫn **flaw** (khuyết điểm) → **flawless** (hoàn hảo). **recall** = *thu hồi sản phẩm* (⚠️ *issue a recall*), khác nghĩa "nhớ lại". **hazard** → **hazardous** (adj); *a safety/fire hazard*. **halt** = dừng (*halt production*). **standard** đi với *meet safety/quality standards*. Họ từ hay ra Part 5: **inspect** → inspector/inspection; **durable** → durability; **precision** → precise. **malfunction** (v/n) ≈ trục trặc; **breakdown** (n) = hỏng hóc. **outsource** = thuê ngoài (đối lập tự làm nội bộ).

## Bài tập TOEIC (tự làm trước, chưa xem đáp án)

### Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences
Chọn phương án đúng (A/B/C/D) điền vào chỗ trống.

**1.** After Ben's warning, the QC team stopped the line to ______ every unit in the batch.
(A) outsource (B) inspect (C) assemble (D) streamline

**2.** What looked at first like a minor ______ turned out to be a systematic problem.
(A) flaw (B) shift (C) capacity (D) warehouse

**3.** The replacement chips were not ______ enough and overheated under heavy use.
(A) fragile (B) durable (C) defective (D) hazardous

**4.** Under stress testing, many units began to ______, freezing or short-circuiting.
(A) inspect (B) malfunction (C) assemble (D) recall

**5.** Because none of the units met the safety ______, Ray refused to ship them.
(A) standard (B) precision (C) warehouse (D) batch

**6.** Overheating components inside a home device pose a serious fire ______.
(A) defect (B) hazard (C) shift (D) breakdown

**7.** Rather than risk faulty products, Ray decided to ______ production immediately.
(A) halt (B) automate (C) streamline (D) inspect

**8.** If defective units reached customers, the company would have to issue a ______.
(A) refund (B) recall (C) surcharge (D) warehouse

**9.** The suspect ______ was sealed off and moved to a corner of the warehouse.
(A) shift (B) batch (C) standard (D) precision

**10.** Workers dismantling the hot units were told to wear ______.
(A) protective gear (B) raw materials (C) a conveyor belt (D) a purchase order

**11.** Sophia examined each board with the ______ of a perfectionist engineer.
(A) capacity (B) precision (C) recall (D) hazard

**12.** Management chose not to ______ the repair, since the root cause lay in the design.
(A) inspect (B) outsource (C) assemble (D) halt

### Part 7 — Reading Comprehension

## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **B (inspect)** — "stopped the line to inspect every unit" = kiểm tra kỹ. *outsource/assemble/streamline* sai nghĩa.
2. **A (flaw)** — "a minor flaw… turned out systematic" = khuyết điểm. *shift/capacity/warehouse* sai nghĩa.
3. **B (durable)** — "not durable enough and overheated under heavy use" = bền. ⚠️ *fragile* = dễ vỡ (không hợp "not… enough"); *defective/hazardous* sai nghĩa.
4. **B (malfunction)** — "freezing or short-circuiting" = trục trặc. *inspect/assemble/recall* sai nghĩa.
5. **A (standard)** — "met the safety standard" = tiêu chuẩn. *precision/warehouse/batch* sai nghĩa.
6. **B (hazard)** — "a serious fire hazard" = mối nguy hiểm. *defect/shift/breakdown* sai nghĩa.
7. **A (halt)** — "halt production immediately" = dừng. *automate/streamline/inspect* sai nghĩa.
8. **B (recall)** — "if defective units reached customers… issue a recall" = thu hồi sản phẩm. ⚠️ *refund* = hoàn tiền (khác); *surcharge/warehouse* sai.
9. **B (batch)** — "the suspect batch was sealed off" = lô sản xuất. *shift/standard/precision* sai nghĩa.
10. **A (protective gear)** — "dismantling the hot units… wear protective gear" = đồ bảo hộ. *raw materials/conveyor belt/purchase order* sai nghĩa.
11. **B (precision)** — "examined each board with the precision of…" = độ chính xác. *capacity/recall/hazard* sai nghĩa.
12. **B (outsource)** — "not… the repair, since the root cause lay in the design" = thuê ngoài. *inspect/assemble/halt* sai nghĩa.

### Part 6
13. **A (quality control)** — "during routine quality control, inspectors found…" = kiểm soát chất lượng. *shipping and handling/word of mouth/collective bargaining* sai ngữ cảnh.
14. **B (standards)** — "does not meet our safety standards" = tiêu chuẩn. *precision/warehouses/batches* sai nghĩa.
15. **A (warehouse)** — "sealed and stored in the warehouse, away from finished goods" = nhà kho. *foreman/shift/invoice* sai nghĩa.
16. **A (protective gear)** — "must wear protective gear… units may overheat" = đồ bảo hộ. *raw materials/an itinerary/a catalog* sai nghĩa.
17. **A (durable)** — "the chips are not durable; under load they overheat" = bền. *vacant/frugal/overdue* sai nghĩa.
18. **B (breakdown)** — "several test units suffered a complete breakdown" = hỏng hóc. *surcharge/discount/shift* sai nghĩa.
19. **B (defect)** — "a genuine defect in the components themselves" = lỗi sản phẩm. *recall/hazard/standard* sai nghĩa.
20. **A (outsource)** — "do not… the redesign to an outside firm" = thuê ngoài. *inspect/assemble/halt* sai nghĩa.

### Part 7
21. **B** — QC phát hiện "a batch… **failed to meet safety standards**". (A) trái; (C)/(D) không nêu.
22. **B** — dừng sản xuất vì "overheating poses a **fire hazard**". (A)/(C)/(D) sai/không nêu.
23. **A** — không thuê ngoài/vá tay được vì "the fault is a genuine **defect in the components**, not an assembly mistake". (B)/(C)/(D) sai.
24. **B** — Aster "**stopped production** of its Aria line just weeks before launch". (A)/(C)/(D) sai/trái.
25. **B** — nguồn lỗi: "**replacement chips** the company rushed in after an earlier **supply failure**". (A)/(C)/(D) sai.
