## Bảng tổng hợp từ vựng

| Từ | Phiên âm (IPA) | Loại | Nghĩa | Collocation thường gặp | Ví dụ câu tiếng Anh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| research | /rɪˈsɜːtʃ/ | n/v | nghiên cứu | conduct research **on** sth ⚠️ **không đếm được** | The team did months of research. |
| development | /dɪˈveləpmənt/ | n | sự phát triển | product development; R&D | Aria's development moved to R&D. |
| innovation | /ˌɪnəˈveɪʃn/ | n | sự đổi mới | drive / foster innovation | The crisis forced real innovation. |
| innovative | /ˈɪnəveɪtɪv/ | adj | mang tính đổi mới | an innovative approach | They took an innovative approach. |
| prototype | /ˈprəʊtətaɪp/ | n | nguyên mẫu | build / test a prototype | They built a new prototype in two days. |
| concept | /ˈkɒnsept/ | n | ý tưởng, khái niệm | a concept design | The concept began on a whiteboard. |
| feasibility study | /ˌfiːzəˈbɪləti ˈstʌdi/ | n | nghiên cứu tính khả thi | carry out a feasibility study | A quick feasibility study said yes. |
| viable | /ˈvaɪəbl/ | adj | khả thi, tồn tại được | a commercially viable product | Was the fix viable in time? |
| pilot | /ˈpaɪlət/ | adj/n | (cuộc) thử nghiệm nhỏ | a pilot program / project | They ran a small pilot first. |
| trial | /ˈtraɪəl/ | n | cuộc thử nghiệm | run trials; clinical trials | Heat trials pushed the units hard. |
| phase | /feɪz/ | n/v | giai đoạn | the testing phase; **phase out** | Testing went in phases. |
| findings | /ˈfaɪndɪŋz/ | n | kết quả nghiên cứu | present / review the findings | They reviewed the sensor findings nightly. |
| refine | /rɪˈfaɪn/ | v | tinh chỉnh | refine the design | They refined the circuit layout. |
| enhance | /ɪnˈhɑːns/ | v | nâng cao | enhance performance | New fins enhanced cooling. |
| modify | /ˈmɒdɪfaɪ/ | v | chỉnh sửa | modify the prototype | They modified the control software. |
| breakthrough | /ˈbreɪkθruː/ | n | bước đột phá | a major breakthrough | The stable run looked like a breakthrough. |
| collaborate | /kəˈlæbəreɪt/ | v | hợp tác | collaborate **with** sb **on** sth | Hardware collaborated with software. |
| expertise | /ˌekspɜːˈtiːz/ | n | chuyên môn | technical expertise ⚠️ **không đếm được** | They drew on veteran expertise. |

> ⚠️ **Bẫy hay gặp:** **research** & **expertise** là danh từ **KHÔNG đếm được** (không *-s*, không *a/an*; dùng *a piece of research*, *much expertise*). Họ từ: **innovation** (n) ↔ **innovative** (adj) — *drive innovation / an innovative approach*. **viable** (khả thi, sống được) gần **feasible** (đã học Lesson 7) nhưng nhấn "thương mại/tồn tại được". **pilot** (thử nghiệm nhỏ) ≈ **trial** (cuộc thử). **phase** = giai đoạn; ⚠️ *phase out* = loại bỏ dần. **findings** luôn ở số nhiều (*present the findings*). Nhóm động từ cải tiến: **refine** (tinh chỉnh nhỏ) · **enhance** (nâng cao) · **modify** (sửa đổi). **collaborate** đi với *with sb on sth*. ⚠️ **breakthrough** (đột phá, n) khác **breakdown** (hỏng hóc — Lesson 18).

## Bài tập TOEIC (tự làm trước, chưa xem đáp án)

### Part 5 — Incomplete Sentences
Chọn phương án đúng (A/B/C/D) điền vào chỗ trống.

**1.** After the recall scare, the whole burden of saving Aria shifted to the ______ and development team.
(A) warehouse (B) recall (C) research (D) shipment

**2.** Instead of patching the old chip, Sophia pushed for real ______ in the power circuit.
(A) innovation (B) inventory (C) surcharge (D) breakdown

**3.** The team took an ______ approach so that no single chip could overheat the whole device.
(A) obsolete (B) overdue (C) durable (D) innovative

**4.** From a rough ______ on the whiteboard, they built a working model in two days.
(A) warehouse (B) concept (C) refund (D) shift

**5.** Engineers built a new ______ to test the redesigned circuit.
(A) prototype (B) invoice (C) surcharge (D) foreman

**6.** A quick ______ confirmed that the redesign was possible within the remaining time and budget.
(A) purchase order (B) clearance sale (C) feasibility study (D) night shift

**7.** The real question was whether the fix would be ______ before the fixed launch date.
(A) fragile (B) viable (C) defective (D) vacant

**8.** Before mass testing, the team ran a small ______ on just a few units.
(A) recall (B) surcharge (C) batch (D) pilot

**9.** Testing was carried out in several ______, from small samples to full heat trials.
(A) shifts (B) invoices (C) phases (D) hazards

**10.** Each night the engineers reviewed the ______ from the temperature sensors.
(A) findings (B) surcharges (C) carriers (D) standards

**11.** After each round, they ______ the circuit layout to improve cooling.
(A) dispatched (B) outsourced (C) halted (D) refined

**12.** Sophia asked the hardware and software groups to ______ closely on the fix.
(A) inspect (B) collaborate (C) expedite (D) recall

## Đáp án & giải thích

### Part 5
1. **C (research)** — "the research and development team" = nghiên cứu. *warehouse/recall/shipment* sai nghĩa.
2. **A (innovation)** — "pushed for real innovation in the power circuit" = đổi mới. *inventory/surcharge/breakdown* sai nghĩa.
3. **D (innovative)** — "an innovative approach" = mang tính đổi mới. *obsolete* (lỗi thời) trái nghĩa; *overdue/durable* sai.
4. **B (concept)** — "a rough concept on the whiteboard" = ý tưởng. *warehouse/refund/shift* sai nghĩa.
5. **A (prototype)** — "built a new prototype to test the circuit" = nguyên mẫu. *invoice/surcharge/foreman* sai nghĩa.
6. **C (feasibility study)** — "confirmed the redesign was possible within time and budget" = nghiên cứu tính khả thi. *purchase order/clearance sale/night shift* sai.
7. **B (viable)** — "whether the fix would be viable before the launch" = khả thi. ⚠️ *fragile/defective/vacant* sai nghĩa.
8. **D (pilot)** — "a small pilot on just a few units" = thử nghiệm nhỏ. *recall/surcharge/batch* sai nghĩa.
9. **C (phases)** — "in several phases, from small samples to heat trials" = giai đoạn. *shifts/invoices/hazards* sai nghĩa.
10. **A (findings)** — "reviewed the findings from the sensors" = kết quả nghiên cứu. *surcharges/carriers/standards* sai nghĩa.
11. **D (refined)** — "refined the circuit layout to improve cooling" = tinh chỉnh. *dispatched/outsourced/halted* sai nghĩa.
12. **B (collaborate)** — "hardware and software groups collaborate closely" = hợp tác. *inspect/expedite/recall* sai nghĩa.

### Part 6
13. **B (development)** — "the product development effort" = sự phát triển. *warehouse/recall/surcharge* sai nghĩa.
14. **D (enhance)** — "redesign it to enhance its cooling and stability" = nâng cao. *outsource/halt/dispatch* sai nghĩa.
15. **A (expertise)** — "draw on the expertise of our senior engineers" = chuyên môn. ⚠️ không đếm được. *inventory/shipment/surplus* sai.
16. **C (modify)** — "modify the design after every round" = chỉnh sửa. *inspect/recall/expedite* sai nghĩa.
17. **B (phase)** — "entered the testing phase" = giai đoạn. *invoice/recall/hazard* sai nghĩa.
18. **D (pilot)** — "a small pilot on a handful of units" = thử nghiệm nhỏ. *refund/foreman/surcharge* sai nghĩa.
19. **C (trials)** — "full heat trials, pushing the devices harder" = cuộc thử nghiệm. *shifts/invoices/standards* sai nghĩa.
20. **A (breakthrough)** — "stayed cool… cautiously calling a breakthrough" = bước đột phá. ⚠️ *breakdown* (hỏng hóc) sai nghĩa; *surcharge/warehouse* sai.

### Part 7
21. **C** — R&D chọn "an **innovative redesign** of the power circuit" thay vì vá. (A)/(B)/(D) sai/trái.
22. **A** — feasibility study xác nhận "the approach was **viable within the remaining schedule**". (B)/(C)/(D) sai.
23. **B** — thử nghiệm "proceeded **in phases, from a small pilot to demanding heat trials**". (A)/(C)/(D) sai.
24. **D** — Aster "fix the problem **in-house** rather than seek help from outside" (đội R&D của mình). (A)/(B)/(C) sai/trái.
25. **A** — có thể đã tìm ra "a **breakthrough that keeps the device cool and stable**". (B)/(C)/(D) sai.
26. **C** — nếu thiết kế trụ được, vẫn phải "**protect the technology and prepare it for mass production**" → dẫn Ch 20 (patent, độc quyền, triển khai). (A)/(B)/(D) sai.
